Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers to common questions about showroom billing and app security.

How do Indian jewellers calculate final price on a bill?
The standard industry billing formula is: Final Price = [(Net Weight + Wastage Weight) × Spot Rate/g] + Making Charges + 3% GST. Many jewellers combine Wastage (Value Addition or VA) and Making Charges into a single percentage markup. Metal Price Estimator computes each component transparently so you know exactly how much is going into gold, craftsmanship, and statutory taxes.
What is the difference between Pavan and Tola?
In South India (Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh), gold is traditionally measured in Pavan (also known as Sovereign), where 1 Pavan equals exactly 8.000 grams. In North India and traditional bullion markets, gold is measured in Tola, where 1 Tola equals 11.6638 grams (or colloquially rounded to 10 grams in metric tola). The app provides automatic dual-unit conversion so you can verify weights regardless of your jeweler's preferred terminology.
Why does Metal Price Estimator require zero internet permissions?
Personal gold, silver, and jewellery holdings represent sensitive household wealth. Metal Price Estimator is built with an absolute air-gapped security model: the Android package has no android.permission.INTERNET declared in its manifest. Your spot prices, portfolio entries, weights, and calculations never leave your device.
How is 3% GST applied on gold purchases?
Under Indian GST regulations, a flat 3% Goods and Services Tax (comprising 1.5% CGST and 1.5% SGST) is levied on the total taxable value of the jewellery—which includes the base metal value plus any wastage and making charges. Metal Price Estimator isolates this statutory tax so you can distinguish between government taxes and showroom markups.
Can I calculate scrap or old gold exchange value?
Yes. When exchanging old gold, showrooms typically melt and test purity, then apply a melting deduction (often 1% to 3%) without charging VA or making charges. You can enter your old gold's weight and purity (e.g. 91.6% for 22K or 75.0% for 18K) to calculate the net exchange credit you should receive against your new purchase.
How does the hardware biometric lock protect my portfolio?
When enabled, the app integrates with Android's BiometricPrompt API using device hardware (Fingerprint scanner or Face Unlock). Your portfolio data remains encrypted on disk and is only accessible after biometric authentication. The app also prevents unauthorized screen captures and masks content in the Android task switcher.
