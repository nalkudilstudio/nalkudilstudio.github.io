Section 01

Retail Billing Estimation Disclaimer

Metal Price Estimator calculates estimates using standard retail jewellery formulas: Effective Weight = Net Weight + Wastage (gm), multiplied by the gold rate plus 3% statutory GST. Actual invoices issued by jewellers may vary based on specific hallmark fees, promotional discounts, stone pricing tiers, or varying making charges.

Section 02

Jeweller Invoicing Discretion

This application does not set or enforce gold rates. Rates entered into the app are user-configured board rates. Nalkudil Studio is not responsible for pricing differences between the app's calculation and a retail jeweller's terminal invoice.

Section 03

Regional Unit Conventions

Conversions between metric Grams and traditional units (1 Sovereign / Pavan = 8.000g, 1 Tola = 11.6638g) are based on standard Indian trade conventions. Minor milligram rounding differences may occur depending on showroom scales.

Section 04

Local Device Storage & Data Preservation

Because the app contains no internet permissions and operates strictly on-device, your saved purchase valuations are stored solely in your local app storage. Please ensure your device is backed up if you wish to retain long-term purchase records.
