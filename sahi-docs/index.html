FAQ

Common Questions

Does Sahi require an internet connection?
No. Sahi is built with a strict Air-Gapped Sovereign Philosophy. The application completely omits android.permission.INTERNET from its manifest. It operates 100% offline, anywhere in the world, with zero connection to the outside web.
Where is my financial and gold locker data stored?
All profiles, loan details, salary allocations, and precious metals gram counts are stored solely in your device's internal SQLite database (Room) inside the app's sandboxed storage. Neither Nalkudil Studio nor any third party can access or read this data.
Are the financial formulas verified against Indian banking standards?
Yes. All formulas are rigorously unit-tested against statutory norms: IBA quarterly compounding for RDs, RBI Master Directions 75% LTV ceilings for gold loans, and Union Budget July 2024 capital gains tax brackets for digital gold.
How does the Biometric Shield protect my numbers?
When enabled, Sahi invokes Android's hardware Keystore via BiometricPrompt upon launch. Furthermore, whenever you switch apps, a lifecycle-aware masking scrim immediately obscures all financial balances in the Android recent apps carousel.
Can I sync my Sahi profile across multiple devices or cloud?
By deliberate architectural design, Sahi does not support cloud sync to ensure absolute data sovereignty. If you wish to migrate phones, you can re-enter your baseline profile in under 60 seconds on the new device.
Is Sahi a SEBI-registered investment advisor?
No. Sahi is a deterministic mathematical calculation utility designed for personal budgeting and estimation. It does not provide personalized investment, legal, or tax advisory services.
