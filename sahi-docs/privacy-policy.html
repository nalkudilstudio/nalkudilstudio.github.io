Privacy Policy

📅 Last updated: October 2026  ·  🏢 Nalkudil Studio  ·  📧 nalkudilstudio@gmail.com

Our Absolute Sovereign Guarantee: Sahi is architected with a strict Air-Gapped Sovereign Philosophy. The application contains zero network permissions (android.permission.INTERNET is strictly absent from its Android manifest). Sahi cannot connect to the internet, cannot send telemetry, contains no ad networks or third-party trackers, and stores 100% of your data exclusively on your physical device.

Section 01

Information We Collect (And What We Never Collect)

Data you enter into Sahi:

  • Cashflow & Income: Monthly take-home pay, statutory EPF/NPS deductions, household essentials, and dependent allocations.
  • Debt Portfolio: Loan balances, interest rates, tenures, and monthly EMIs (Home loan, Car loan, Personal loan, Gold loan, Education loan).
  • Precious Metals Vault: Locker gram quantities (22K gold, 24K bullion, silver, platinum) and DigiGold/SGB units.
  • Family Context: Age, target retirement age, senior citizen parent flag, and dependent counts.

Where does this data go?

Every single piece of information you input remains strictly confined to your device's internal SQLite database (Room). Neither Nalkudil Studio, nor any cloud server, nor any external party can ever view, intercept, or retrieve your profile.

Section 02

Zero Network Permission Mandate

Unlike standard fintech and banking apps, Sahi does not ask for or use the android.permission.INTERNET permission. You can verify this independently at any time in Android Settings > Apps > Sahi > Permissions.

  • No remote servers or cloud databases exist for Sahi.
  • No analytics SDKs (no Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or CleverTap).
  • No crash-reporting network beacons (no Sentry, Bugsnag, or Crashlytics).
  • No advertising identifiers (AAID) or tracking cookies.

Section 04

Third-Party Services & Trackers

Sahi integrates zero third-party software development kits (SDKs) that transmit data over networks. The application is completely self-contained. All calculation engines (Salary Planner, Home Budget, Retirement, Gold Price, Gold Loan, DigiGold, FD, RD, Home Loan Prepayment) are written in pure Kotlin algorithms executing directly on your phone's CPU.

Section 05

Client-Side Graphic Sharing

When you choose to export or share a financial health summary card or quote via WhatsApp or message apps, the card image is rendered completely on-device using Android's native Canvas engine into your private temporary cache directory. The file is handed over to your chosen app via Android's secure FileProvider. Sahi has no visibility into where or to whom you send the image.

Section 06

Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not store your data on any server, you maintain complete sovereignty over your data at all times:

  • You can reset or wipe all entered numbers from the Profile settings screen inside the app.
  • Clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app permanently deletes 100% of your records immediately.
Section 07

Children's Privacy

Sahi is an adult financial planning utility. We do not knowingly collect or solicit data from children under 13 (or under 18 in relevant jurisdictions). The app does not transmit any information externally under any circumstance.

Section 08

Statutory Compliance (DPDP Act 2023)

In accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), Sahi embodies the highest standard of Privacy by Design. Because no personal data is transferred off the data principal's hardware, no cross-border processing occurs, and zero profiling or surveillance takes place.

Section 09

Changes to this Policy

If we update our offline architectural guarantees in a major release, we will provide an in-app changelog notification. The 'Last Updated' timestamp at the top of this document always indicates the operative release version.
