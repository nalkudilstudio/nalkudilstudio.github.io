Statutory Formulas & Tax Calculations

Sahi incorporates publicly established Indian banking and tax standards, including:

  • RBI Master Directions: 75% LTV ceilings and conservative valuations on gold loans.
  • Indian Banks' Association (IBA): Quarterly compounding for Recurring Deposits.
  • Union Budget July 2024 Tax Norms: 12.5% LTCG on DigiGold/SGB and revised tax brackets.
  • Income Tax Act: Section 194A TDS on Fixed Deposits and Section 80C exemptions.

While formulas are verified through rigorous unit tests, tax rules are subject to legislative change. Users must verify calculations against official gazettes.
